Proposals turns your offers into designed sales documents. Instead of a form with line items, you write the whole offer in a full-page block editor, present the price where it makes the case, and let the customer accept it by signing online.

## Write the offer, not a form

- **Full-page block editor** with headings, lists, images and other blocks, edited directly on the proposal
- **Design settings** for the accent colour, backgrounds, text colours and fonts, so every proposal looks like your brand
- **Merge tags** that fill in the customer's name, billing address and your company details
- **For contacts, companies or leads**, with a sale agent and an expiry date

## Pricing and acceptance

- **Line items block** that puts the pricing anywhere in the document, with quantities, rates, taxes and totals
- **Optional items** the customer can select on the public page
- **Signature block** where the customer accepts by typing their name; the PDF records who accepted and when
- **Attachments** the customer can download from the public page

## Works like your quotes and estimates

Proposals builds on oneCRM's documents: sending, public links, expiry reminders, accepting and declining, converting to an invoice, permissions and reports all work the same way. The PDF follows the same layout as the public page, and an accepted proposal is locked, so the offer the customer signed stays exactly as it was.
