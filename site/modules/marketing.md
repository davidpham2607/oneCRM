Email marketing built into oneCRM. Send campaigns to the leads, contacts and newsletter subscribers already in your CRM, run sequences that follow up on their own, keep proof of consent, and see which emails brought in deals and paid invoices.

## Campaigns that report back

- Send to audiences now or at a set time, as a designed email or a plain personal one with your signature
- Opens, clicks, replies, bounces and unsubscribes for every campaign, and a follow-up draft for everyone who did not click
- Revenue credit for won deals and paid invoices (with Deals or Invoices), so you see what each email earned

## Sequences that follow up for you

- Emails with waits between them, for people you add, everyone in an audience, or each new web form signup
- Ready recipes to welcome subscribers, follow up web form leads, nurture leads, onboard customers and win back inactive subscribers
- Stop rules that end a sequence when someone clicks, replies or becomes a customer

## Consent you can prove

- One marketing status per email address, shared by leads, contacts and subscribers
- Signup forms with double opt-in, imports with a legal basis, and a full consent history
- An unsubscribe link and your postal address in every email, and campaigns that pause themselves when bounces or spam complaints run high

## Your brand, your sending

Set your colours, font and footer once in the brand kit. Send through your own Postmark, Mailgun or Resend account, and see the website pages people visit with the optional website tracker.
