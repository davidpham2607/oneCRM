oneCRM ("we", "us") operates onecrm.example.com. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal data when you visit our website, create an account, purchase a licence, or contact support.

## Your self-hosted CRM and this website

We are responsible for the personal data we use to operate this website, manage licences, and provide support. The CRM you install runs on your chosen server. Buying a licence does not give us routine access to your customer database or make us the host of your CRM records.

You decide how personal data in your installation is used and which hosting, email, payment, and AI services you connect. You are responsible for the privacy notices and permissions required for that use. People asking about data held by one of our customers should contact that organisation.

Your installation can send us information for licence registration, verification, downloads, updates, and bug reports. We also receive any records or access details you choose to share for support, as explained below.

## Data we collect

- Account information: name, email address, and password (stored hashed).
- Purchase and licence data: order references, licence keys, product entitlements, and support status.
- Installation data: licence key, installation identifier, application URL, environment, optional label, URL changes, and the time of licence checks.
- Support data: your name, email, messages, attachments, product details, and any access credentials you choose to provide through the secure support form.
- Bug reports: the description and diagnostics submitted from your CRM, which may include your installation URL, software and PHP versions, system information, logs, reporter details, and IP address. Status emails are optional when you submit a report.
- Envato connection: if you sign in with Envato, we receive your account identifier, username, email, and authorisation tokens, and retrieve purchase and support-entitlement information to verify your licences.
- Technical data: IP address, browser type, and server logs for security and diagnostics.

## Payment data

Paddle is the Merchant of Record for paid website orders and handles payment, tax, and billing information under its own privacy policy. We receive the buyer and transaction information needed to deliver your purchase, manage licences, and handle support or disputes. We do not receive or store full payment-card details.

[Paddle Privacy Policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy)

## Cookies

We use cookies for sessions, sign-in, and form security. The documentation also uses browser storage to remember navigation preferences. Blocking necessary cookies may prevent account and checkout features from working.

We use Google Analytics to measure page visits, product and demo clicks, checkout activity, and purchases. This can include device and browser information, approximate location, product identifiers, transaction references, and purchase values. Google may use cookies and processes this information under its privacy policy.

[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

Paddle checkout and external services you visit may use their own cookies under their policies. Your browser lets you manage stored cookies and site data; this does not remove information already received by those services.

## How we use your data

- To provide accounts, licences, downloads, and support.
- To send transactional emails such as purchase confirmations and ticket updates.
- To send marketing emails about new releases and offers, as described below.
- To prevent fraud, enforce our terms, and maintain security.
- To measure website traffic and improve our pages via Google Analytics.

## Marketing emails

We may email you about new releases, product updates, and offers for oneCRM and its modules. We send these to customers who hold a licence and to anyone who opted in, for example by ticking the box when creating an account. Accounts moved from our previous customer portal receive them too.

Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing does not affect transactional emails about your purchases, licences, or support. Marketing emails are delivered by our email provider, Postmark, which also keeps the list of addresses that unsubscribed, bounced, or reported an email as spam so we stop sending to them.

## Legal bases

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we use information needed to fulfil our contract with you, including accounts, licences, delivery, and support, or to answer questions before a purchase. We also process information to meet applicable legal obligations and for our legitimate interests in securing the website, preventing licence abuse, investigating faults, resolving disputes, and telling customers about products similar to those they own. Where consent is required for optional processing, that processing requires your consent, which you can withdraw without affecting earlier lawful use.

You can browse without creating an account. If you do not provide information needed for a purchase, licence check, or support request, we may be unable to provide that service.

## Support, diagnostics, and AI

Please remove passwords, payment details, and unrelated customer information from messages, screenshots, and logs. Use the secure credentials feature if access is needed for support. Providing access authorises us to investigate the reported issue; it does not transfer responsibility for your installation or backups to us.

When AI-assisted support is enabled, ticket subjects and messages can be sent to our configured AI and search providers to find relevant documentation and suggest an answer. The support system supports OpenAI or Google Gemini for this work and Cohere for ranking search results. Bug-report details and logs may also be reviewed with AI-assisted development tools when investigating a fix. AI suggestions can be wrong; you can continue the support conversation with our team.

## Sharing

We use providers for hosting, file storage, email delivery, security, and diagnostics. Information is also shared with Paddle for orders, Envato for connected accounts and licence checks, and AI providers when those support features are used. We do not sell your personal data.

For a third-party module support request, the relevant module vendor and its authorised support staff can access the ticket information needed to help you. We may also disclose information where required by law or needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

When Google Analytics is enabled, usage data is processed by Google as a service provider.

## International processing

Our providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, those requirements apply to our use of the provider. Contact us for information about the providers, processing locations, and transfer safeguards relevant to your data.

## Retention

We keep account, purchase, and licence records as needed to recognise your continuing licence rights, deliver services, meet record-keeping obligations, and resolve disputes. Closing an account does not necessarily remove records we must retain for these purposes.

Support messages, attachments, and bug reports are kept for handling the issue, related follow-up, and resolving claims. Technical logs are kept for security and fault investigation. The retention period depends on the purpose, whether the issue remains open, and any applicable legal requirements. You can ask us to review or delete data that is no longer needed.

## Your rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a portable copy of your personal data, restrict processing, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent. Submit a request through our [Contact](/site/contact) page; you do not need an active support licence to make a privacy request.

We may ask for information needed to verify your identity and will respond within the period required by applicable law. Some rights have legal exceptions. You may also complain to the data-protection authority responsible for your country or region.

Privacy questions or data subject requests: [Contact us](/site/contact).
