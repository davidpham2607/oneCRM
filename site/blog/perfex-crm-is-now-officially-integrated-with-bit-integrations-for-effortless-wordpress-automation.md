### What is Bit Integrations?

[Bit Integrations](https://bit-integrations.com/) lets you seamlessly automate workflows between your WordPress site and 291+ platforms - no coding required. oneCRM has quickly become one of the most-requested integrations, and now it’s finally here.

### What Can You Do with the Bit Integrations + oneCRM Integration?

- **Capture Leads Instantly:** Connect your WordPress forms (including Elementor, Contact Form 7, Gravity Forms, and more) directly to oneCRM. Every new lead is automatically sent to your CRM - no manual exports or import headaches.
- **Automate Customer Journeys:** Trigger actions in oneCRM based on user activity on your WordPress site - perfect for sales pipelines, ticketing, and automated follow-ups.
- **Sync Data Effortlessly:** Bit Integrations supports two-way data exchange, ensuring your CRM and website always stay in sync.
