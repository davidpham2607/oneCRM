Our products are digital downloads delivered immediately. Once delivered, purchases are non-refundable except where required by law and in the limited circumstances described below.

## Website purchases

For digital products purchased directly on this website, refund requests submitted within 14 days of the order completion date are reviewed case-by-case. Approval is not automatic.

To request a refund, [contact us](https://onecrm.example.com/contact) with your order reference. If you have an active support licence, sign in and open a support ticket for faster handling.

## EU right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer in the EU/EEA, you normally have a 14-day right of withdrawal for distance purchases. For digital content supplied immediately, this right does not apply once supply has begun if you have given prior express consent and acknowledged that you lose the right of withdrawal.

By purchasing and downloading, you request immediate performance and acknowledge that you thereby lose your 14-day right of withdrawal.

## When we may still issue a refund

- You were charged more than once, or charged in error, for the same order.
- The product was never delivered and we are unable to deliver it within a reasonable time.
- The product has a material defect that makes it substantially unusable, you have reported it with enough detail for us to reproduce it, and we are unable to provide a fix or workaround within a reasonable time.

Refunds are not provided for change of mind, accidental purchases, lack of technical expertise, compatibility issues that were stated before purchase, or expectations not explicitly advertised on the product page.

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. We may verify the issue and require that you cease using and delete the product as a condition of the refund.

## Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits or excludes any rights you have under mandatory consumer-protection law, including remedies for digital content that is faulty, not as described, or not fit for purpose. Those statutory rights always apply in addition to this policy.

## Billing and Merchant of Record

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders and handles payment, tax, and billing enquiries.

Approved refunds are settled through Paddle, and Paddle's buyer terms also apply to your transaction.

## Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is wrong, please [contact us](https://onecrm.example.com/contact) first  -  we can usually resolve it faster than your bank. Initiating a chargeback or payment dispute without contacting us may result in suspension or termination of the related licence while the dispute is investigated.

## Digital goods

Because our products are delivered electronically, we recommend reviewing product documentation and system requirements before extensive use. The refund window begins when your order completes and your licence key is issued.

## CodeCanyon purchases

oneCRM Classic and marketplace items purchased through Envato CodeCanyon are subject to Envato's refund policy, not this policy.

## After the refund period

Orders outside the review period are generally non-refundable. Chargebacks may incur fees and should be avoided  -  contact us first to resolve billing issues.

## Contact

Refund requests: [Contact us](https://onecrm.example.com/contact). Billing disputes: [Paddle buyer support](https://paddle.net).
