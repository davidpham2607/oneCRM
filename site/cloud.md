How is it different from the self-hosted edition? It is the same oneCRM with every feature. We run the server, apply updates and patches, take daily backups, and keep email, real-time updates and scheduled tasks running.

Can I use my own domain? Yes, a subdomain such as crm.yourcompany.com. Point a CNAME record at the name we give you and HTTPS is set up automatically. Your first address keeps working and sends visitors on to the new one.

How are backups handled? Your database and files are backed up every day, at night in your CRM's timezone, and kept for four weeks in a separate location from your CRM's server. Updates are applied at night too. Ask us and we restore a backup for you.

What happens when I need more users or storage? Each plan covers a number of users and storage for your files and database together. We email you as you get close to the limit. At the limit new uploads stop until you free space or upgrade, and everything else keeps working.

Can I install modules? Yes, modules bought on onecrm.example.com. Download the module from your account and upload that zip as it is. Modules from other sources cannot be installed.

Can I send email through my own provider? Email works from the first day. You can switch to your own SMTP server or email provider in the settings at any time.

Do Gmail and Outlook work? Yes. Connect them in one click, with no Google or Microsoft app to register. Using your own app is still an option.

Can I take my data with me? Your data is yours. Ask us and we send your database and files, ready to run on your own server.
