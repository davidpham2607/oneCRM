## Catch every enquiry while you are with a client

Put a lead form on your website and each submission arrives as a new lead, with a check for duplicates. When the lead is worth pursuing, convert it into a contact, company, and deal in one step.

- Embeddable lead forms for your own website
- Board and table views that show who still needs a reply
- A deal in your pipeline without retyping the details

## Let the routine follow-up run without you

Nobody else will send the thank-you email after a payment or the check-in after a deal is won. Set a rule once and the CRM sends it, straight away or after a delay you choose.

- Rules that start when a deal is won or a payment is recorded
- Emails sent for you, with the contact's details filled in
- Start from a template or describe the rule to AI

## Get paid without writing the awkward reminder

Send an invoice your client can pay online through Stripe, PayPal, or Mollie. Due-date reminders and overdue notices go out automatically, and a recurring invoice covers the clients you bill every month.

- Online payment from the invoice link
- Automatic due and overdue reminders
- Recurring invoices for regular clients

## Buy it once and keep your client list

oneCRM is sold with a one-time licence that includes a period of updates and support, and your install keeps working after that. You run it on your own hosting, which you set up and pay for yourself or with help from your hosting provider.

- One-time purchase with no monthly CRM subscription
- Client records and invoices on a server you choose
- Renew when you want new releases and support again
