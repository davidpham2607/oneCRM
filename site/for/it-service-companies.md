A new support client needs more than a welcome email. Build an onboarding project with tasks for confirming contacts, planning the installation, and sharing support instructions. Assign each step before the work starts.

## Every client request in one organised queue

A client reports an email problem while another needs help connecting remotely. Assign each ticket and keep the conversation and troubleshooting notes with it. The next technician can see what has already been tried.

- Client emails collected as support tickets
- Ticket assignments and statuses for the service team
- Internal notes for troubleshooting and handovers

## Help clients help themselves

Write down the steps for common questions, such as setting up email or connecting to the office VPN. Share client-facing guides in the knowledge base and keep internal troubleshooting articles for your team.

- Setup guides clients can follow in their own time
- Internal articles for fixes your technicians reuse

## Send the monthly invoice without starting again

Monthly support and a one-off installation need different billing. Schedule recurring invoices for the agreed service fee, then quote and invoice separate project work from the same customer record.

- Recurring invoices for monthly support agreements
- Separate quotes and invoices for installation projects
- Invoice history clients can access in their portal
