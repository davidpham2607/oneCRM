## Keep the next enquiry warm while you finish the job

An enquiry arrives while you are working to a deadline. Save what the prospect needs and set a follow-up task. When you have a moment, pick up the conversation and send your quote.

- Notes on the brief, budget, and next conversation

- A follow-up task with a due date

- Quotes prospects can accept when they are ready

## Know what is due before a client asks

Three clients can mean three competing deadlines. Break their jobs into tasks and checklists, keep notes with each project, and see what needs your attention when you switch between them.

- Due dates for each client deliverable

- Checklists to resume a job without retracing your steps

- Files and working notes kept with the right project

## Remember the quick fixes when it is time to invoice

A short call, a revision, and a quick fix can add up. For hourly work, use a timer or log the time afterwards. Build your invoice from those records and check which payments are still due.

- Timers and manual entries for short pieces of work

- Recorded billable time ready for invoicing

- Payment status so you know who still owes you
