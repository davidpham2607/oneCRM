### Choose oneCRM if…

- You bill clients for projects and time, and need quotes, invoices and expenses in the CRM.

- You want a ticket desk, knowledge base and client portal without paying per seat.

- You need customer data on your own server, or EN 16931 e-invoices and VeriFactu.

- Your team is growing and per-seat plans with onboarding fees add up.

### Choose HubSpot if…

- Inbound marketing, email campaigns and sales sequences are central to how you grow.

- You do not want to run a server, backups or updates.

- You need native mobile apps and a large integration marketplace.

- The free CRM for up to 2 users covers what you need.

### Moving from HubSpot

There is no official HubSpot importer. Export contacts, companies and deals from HubSpot as CSV and use the oneCRM CSV import for leads, customers and contacts.

Is HubSpot CRM free? The free CRM covers up to 2 users with contacts, deals, basic tickets and invoices. Quotes, a full help desk, a knowledge base and a customer portal need paid Professional plans priced per seat.

Is oneCRM cheaper than HubSpot? For client work, usually by a wide margin: oneCRM is a one-time licence with no per-seat fees. You do pay for your own hosting, and you look after updates and backups yourself.

Does oneCRM do marketing automation? With the separately sold Marketing module: email campaigns, automated sequences with stop rules, and revenue credited to the email that earned it. oneCRM has no landing pages, and its built-in automations work on records such as leads, tickets and invoices.

Can I host HubSpot myself? No. HubSpot runs only in its own cloud, with one data region per account.
