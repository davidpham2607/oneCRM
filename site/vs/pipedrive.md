### Choose oneCRM if…

- After the deal is won you also quote, invoice, run projects and support the client.

- You want web forms, quotes and projects without add-ons on every seat.

- You need customer data on your own server, or EN 16931 e-invoices and VeriFactu.

- Your team is growing and per-seat plans add up year after year.

### Choose Pipedrive if…

- Your team only sells, and invoicing lives in Xero or QuickBooks.

- You do not want to run a server, backups or updates.

- Polished native mobile apps matter to your sales team.

- You want a large marketplace of sales integrations.

### Moving from Pipedrive

There is no official Pipedrive importer. Export leads, deals, organizations and people from Pipedrive as CSV and use the oneCRM CSV import for leads, deals, companies and contacts.

Does oneCRM have leads and deals like Pipedrive? Yes. Leads are qualified first and converted into a contact, company and deal in one step. Deals move through pipelines with stage probabilities, rotting, products, a weighted forecast and lost reasons.

Is oneCRM cheaper than Pipedrive? For more than a couple of users, usually: oneCRM is a one-time licence with no per-seat fees, and web forms, quotes and projects are included. You do pay for your own hosting, and you look after updates and backups yourself.

Can Pipedrive send invoices? Through its Xero and QuickBooks integrations, which need a paid account with that accounting app. oneCRM creates invoices, takes online payments and sends e-invoices itself.

Can I host Pipedrive myself? No. Pipedrive runs only in its own cloud.
