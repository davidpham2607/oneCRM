# oneCRM vs Invoice Ninja

Invoice Ninja is excellent, free self-hosted invoicing with native apps. Pick it if billing is all you need. oneCRM adds the rest of client work  -  leads and deals, support tickets, a knowledge base and modules  -  and includes Peppol and VeriFactu without a separate licence.

### Choose oneCRM if…

- You need leads, deal pipelines and web forms next to your invoices.
- Clients send support requests and you want tickets and a knowledge base in the same app.
- You want to extend the app with modules, or run on PostgreSQL.
- You need Peppol or VeriFactu without a yearly licence on top.

### Choose Invoice Ninja if…

- Invoicing, payments and subscriptions are all you need.
- You want native desktop and mobile apps.
- You want to pay nothing upfront and are comfortable with the Elastic License.
- You need purchase orders, vendors or bank transaction matching.

### Moving from Invoice Ninja

There is no official Invoice Ninja importer. Export clients and contacts from Invoice Ninja as CSV and import them into oneCRM as customers and contacts. Keep past invoices in Invoice Ninja, or as PDF exports, for your records.
