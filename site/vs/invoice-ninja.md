# oneCRM vs Invoice Ninja

### Choose oneCRM if…

- You need leads, deal pipelines and web forms next to your invoices.

- Clients send support requests and you want tickets and a knowledge base in the same app.

- You want to extend the app with modules, or run on PostgreSQL.

- You need Peppol or VeriFactu without a yearly licence on top.

### Choose Invoice Ninja if…

- Invoicing, payments and subscriptions are all you need.

- You want native desktop and mobile apps.

- You want to pay nothing upfront and are comfortable with the Elastic License.

- You need purchase orders, vendors or bank transaction matching.

### Moving from Invoice Ninja

There is no official Invoice Ninja importer. Export clients and contacts from Invoice Ninja as CSV and import them into oneCRM as customers and contacts. Keep past invoices in Invoice Ninja, or as PDF exports, for your records.

Is self-hosted Invoice Ninja free? Yes. The self-hosted version includes all features at no cost. A $40 per year white-label licence removes Invoice Ninja branding from client-facing pages and is required to send over Peppol.

Is Invoice Ninja open source? Its source code is public under the Elastic License 2.0. That licence is source-available rather than OSI open source: it does not allow offering the software to others as a hosted service.

Does oneCRM have a mobile app? No. oneCRM runs in the browser and works on phones, but there is no native app. Invoice Ninja offers native desktop and mobile apps.

Which one handles e-invoicing? Both create EN 16931 e-invoices. oneCRM sends over Peppol through your own access point account and also supports VeriFactu in Spain. Invoice Ninja sends over Peppol through its own service with prepaid credits.
