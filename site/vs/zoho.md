### Choose oneCRM if…

- You want leads, quotes, projects, invoices, tickets and the client portal in one app, not five.

- Your team is growing and per-user subscriptions add up.

- You need customer data on your own server or in a specific country.

- You need EN 16931 e-invoices, Peppol or VeriFactu next to your CRM.

### Choose Zoho if…

- You do not want to run a server, backups or updates.

- You need native mobile apps and a large integration marketplace.

- You want marketing email, analytics or accounting from the same vendor.

- Up to 3 users on the free CRM plan is enough.

### Moving from Zoho

There is no official Zoho importer. Export leads, accounts and contacts from Zoho CRM as CSV and use the oneCRM CSV import for leads, customers and contacts. Keep invoice history in Zoho or as PDF exports.

Is oneCRM cheaper than Zoho? Over a few years, usually yes: oneCRM is a one-time licence with no per-user fees, while Zoho charges per user per month. You do pay for your own hosting, and you look after updates and backups yourself.

Does one Zoho plan cover CRM, invoicing, projects and help desk? Zoho CRM alone does not include projects or a help desk. You combine Zoho CRM with Zoho Projects, Zoho Desk and Zoho Books or Invoice, or buy the Zoho One bundle.

Can I host Zoho CRM myself? No. Zoho CRM runs only in Zoho's cloud. You choose the data center region when you sign up.

Does oneCRM have a mobile app? No. oneCRM runs in the browser and works on phones, but there is no native app. Zoho has native apps for its products.
