# oneCRM vs Bitrix24

<div v-pre>


Bitrix24 is an all-in-one workspace with chat, video, CRM and tasks at a flat price per team. Pick it if internal collaboration matters as much as client work. oneCRM is focused on client work and billing, with a dedicated ticket desk, client portal and e-invoicing, for a one-time price on your own server.

### Choose oneCRM if…

- Your focus is client work: quotes, projects, billing, support tickets and a client portal.
- You want a dedicated ticket desk rather than tickets built from chat channels.
- You prefer a one-time licence to a monthly plan, and want to run on ordinary PHP hosting.
- You need EN 16931 e-invoices, Peppol or VeriFactu.

### Choose Bitrix24 if…

- You want team chat, video calls and an intranet in the same app.
- You prefer a flat team price with hosting, backups and updates handled for you.
- You need native mobile apps.
- A free plan for one or two people is enough for now.

### Moving from Bitrix24

There is no official Bitrix24 importer. Export leads, companies and contacts from Bitrix24 CRM as CSV and use the oneCRM CSV import for leads, customers and contacts.
