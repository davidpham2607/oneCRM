### Choose oneCRM if…

- Your focus is client work: quotes, projects, billing, support tickets and a client portal.

- You want a dedicated ticket desk rather than tickets built from chat channels.

- You prefer a one-time licence to a monthly plan, and want to run on ordinary PHP hosting.

- You need EN 16931 e-invoices, Peppol or VeriFactu.

### Choose Bitrix24 if…

- You want team chat, video calls and an intranet in the same app.

- You prefer a flat team price with hosting, backups and updates handled for you.

- You need native mobile apps.

- A free plan for one or two people is enough for now.

### Moving from Bitrix24

There is no official Bitrix24 importer. Export leads, companies and contacts from Bitrix24 CRM as CSV and use the oneCRM CSV import for leads, customers and contacts.

Is Bitrix24 priced per user? No. Bitrix24 cloud plans have a flat monthly price with a user limit: for example, Basic covers 5 users and Standard 50. oneCRM has no per-user fees at all.

Can I self-host Bitrix24? Yes. Bitrix24 On-Premise starts at $3,590 per year for 50 users and has to be renewed to keep running. oneCRM is a one-time licence; renewing only adds updates and support.

Does oneCRM have team chat? No. oneCRM has comments and notifications on records, but no chat or video calls. Bitrix24 includes both.

Which one has a better help desk? oneCRM has a dedicated ticket desk with mailboxes, canned responses, a knowledge base and a client portal. Bitrix24 turns emails and chats into tickets through Open Channels.
