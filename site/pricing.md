Is this a subscription? No. You pay once and the licence is yours to keep. 1 year of updates and support is included.

What happens when the 1 year ends? Your CRM keeps running. Renew whenever you want new releases and support again: another 1 year costs $89. Renew before it ends, or within 30 days after, and the new period continues from the end date. Renew later and it starts on the day you pay.

How many installations does a licence cover? One live CRM on one domain, plus as many development and staging copies as you need on that domain or its subdomains. Each additional live domain needs its own licence.

Are there per-user fees? No. Add as many staff members and customers as your server can handle. The price stays the same.

Can I use only the parts I need? Yes. Every licence includes all the built-in modules, such as leads, deals, projects, and tickets. Disable the ones you do not use in Settings, and enable them again later with their data intact. How to turn modules on and off →

Are marketplace modules included? No. Modules from the marketplace are sold separately, and each paid module licence is used with one oneCRM licence.

Can I try it before I buy? Yes. The demo has every oneCRM feature, so you can try it before you buy. Try the demo →

Where can I buy oneCRM? Only on this website. Your licence key and download are ready in your account as soon as the payment completes.

I use oneCRM Classic. Can I move to oneCRM? oneCRM is a separate licence. The free official migrator module moves your data from oneCRM Classic. Third-party oneCRM Classic modules do not run on oneCRM unchanged, so check that the ones you rely on have a oneCRM version. How to move from oneCRM Classic →

Who handles payment and tax? Paddle processes the order as our Merchant of Record and handles billing and sales tax, including VAT.

Can I get a refund? Refund requests sent within 14 days of your order are reviewed case by case. The refund policy explains when a refund is given. Read the refund policy →

I do not want to run a server. Is there a hosted option? Soon. oneCRM Cloud is the same CRM, hosted and updated by us, with daily backups and a monthly or yearly plan. Join the waitlist and we email you when it opens. Join the waitlist →
