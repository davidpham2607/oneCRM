We aim to resolve issues fairly and promptly. If you are unhappy with our product or support, please follow the steps below.

## How to complain

- Submit details through our Contact page, including your order reference if applicable.

- If you have an active support licence, sign in and open a support ticket so we can track your case.

- We aim to acknowledge complaints within 14 business days and provide a substantive response as soon as practicable.

## Billing complaints

For payment, receipt, or subscription billing issues on website orders, contact Paddle buyer support  -  Paddle is the Merchant of Record for our direct sales.

## Escalation

If you remain dissatisfied after our response, reply to your ticket or contact thread with further details. We will review escalated cases internally.
