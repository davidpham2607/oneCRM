What is a self-hosted CRM? A self-hosted CRM, or on-premise CRM, is customer relationship management software you install on your own server or VPS. Instead of logging into a vendor's multi-tenant cloud, you run the application yourself  -  so contacts, invoices, projects, and support history stay on infrastructure you control.

What can I manage with oneCRM? Manage enquiries, customer details, quotes, invoices, projects, and support requests. Your team can follow the work from the first conversation to payment.

Who is self-hosting a good fit for? It suits teams that want to choose where customer data lives and can manage a server or arrange help. You control access, backups, and the timing of updates.

How is self-hosted CRM different from SaaS CRM? SaaS CRMs charge a recurring subscription  -  often per user  -  and store your data on the vendor's servers. A self-hosted CRM like oneCRM is licensed once, runs on your hardware, and does not add per-seat fees as your team grows.

Will I need help setting it up? You need hosting that meets the requirements for your edition. oneCRM includes a guided installer and setup documentation. Ask a developer or hosting provider for help if you do not manage servers.

What does the licence include? oneCRM is sold with a one-time licence that includes 1 year of updates and support. After that, your install keeps working  -  renew when you want fresh releases and support again.

Can I keep using oneCRM after the support period ends? Yes. Your self-hosted CRM install continues to run after the included support period. You simply will not receive new product updates or support until you renew or purchase a new licence.
