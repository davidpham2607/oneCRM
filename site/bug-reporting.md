### Patches for small fixes

For many smaller issues, we publish a patch customers can apply from their CRM.

### Visible progress

Know whether the issue is open, being worked on, or closed.

Who can submit a bug report? Licensed customers can submit a report directly from their CRM. The report includes the details our team needs to investigate the issue in context.

Will I receive updates about my report? Yes. When submitting a report, customers can opt in to email notifications when its status changes, so they know when it is being investigated or has been resolved.

Will every bug receive a patch? We review every report, but the right resolution depends on the issue and its impact. For many smaller issues, we publish a patch that customers can apply from the CRM updater when it is ready.

How do I apply a published patch? When a patch is available, customers can apply it from Settings to Updater in their CRM admin panel. The update notification includes the relevant patch information.
