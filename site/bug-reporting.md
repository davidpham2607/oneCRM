# A direct line from your CRM to the people fixing it

Report an issue without leaving your CRM, follow its progress, and know when a small fix is ready to install.

Version, environment, and diagnostics attached

### Send the details while they are still fresh

Licensed customers can send a bug report directly from their CRM with the installed version, environment, and diagnostic details our team needs to investigate. That means less back-and-forth before work can begin.

- Report issues without leaving the CRM
- Include version, environment, and diagnostic details
- Keep the report tied to the affected installation

Contact support

Tell us what you need help with and we will get back to you.

Open to in progress to closed  -  with a patch

### Subscribe once, then hear when the report moves forward

Customers can opt in to email updates when a report changes status. For many smaller issues, we publish a patch so the fix can be applied from the CRM updater as soon as it is available.

- Optional email notifications for status changes
- Clear progress from open to in progress to closed
- Patch availability included when a fix is published
