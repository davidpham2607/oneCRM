# We build self-hosted CRM software for client businesses

We help agencies, consultants, and service teams manage client work in one place, on a server they choose.

oneCRM started as a practical CRM for freelancers and small agencies who wanted more control than hosted tools could offer. Today more than 40,000+ teams run it on their own servers to manage clients, invoices, projects, and support tickets in one place.

oneCRM Classic remains the mature CodeIgniter edition  -  trusted by buyers who want a proven, extensible CRM, and available on this website or CodeCanyon. oneCRM is our modern rebuild on Laravel: AI, no-code automations, a branded customer portal, and direct sales and support from this website.
