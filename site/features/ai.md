- Scores and next actions are suggestions. They sit on the record beside the history, and your team decides what to do with them.

Runs on infrastructure you control  -  self-hosted CRM with a one-time licence. Your records only leave your server when a feature you turned on runs.

Which AI providers can I use? Any text provider the settings page lists, such as OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini, with your own API key. Keys are stored encrypted, and some providers also accept a custom API URL.

Do I pay extra for AI? Not to us. AI is part of oneCRM. You pay your provider for the requests your team makes, and you can choose the cheapest model to keep that low.

Does every feature run on the same key? Drafts, summaries, scoring, and the other writing features run on your text provider. Two capabilities need a second key: suggesting knowledge base articles on tickets uses a reranking or embeddings provider, and sorting inbound leads and tickets uses a classification provider. The settings page warns you when a key is missing.

Where does my data go? To the provider you chose, from your own server, only when a feature you turned on runs. Nothing passes through oneCRM, and there is no AI without a key you added.

Can I limit AI to some people or some features? Yes. Every capability has its own switch, and staff need the matching permission on their role. You can give drafting to support and leave everything else off.
