- **Drafts land in the editor.** A reply, article, or description is written where you would type it. Nothing goes out until you send it.
- **Scores and next actions are suggestions.** They sit on the record beside the history, and your team decides what to do with them.
- **Spam leads are flagged, not deleted.** A lead marked as spam stays in the CRM with the result on its record, so a wrong call is easy to undo.
- **Sentiment and urgency inform, they do not override.** They show how the customer feels and how soon they need a reply, without changing the priority your staff set.
