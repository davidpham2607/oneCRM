Is Proposals included in oneCRM? No. Proposals is a separately sold module from the oneCRM marketplace. It installs into your oneCRM, and each module licence is used with one Next licence.

How is a proposal different from a quote or an estimate? Quotes and estimates are short priced lists built in a form. A proposal is a full written offer built in a block editor: your introduction, scope, timeline, pricing, and terms in one designed document, with the price block placed wherever you want it.

How do customers accept a proposal? They open the proposal link, tick any optional items they want, and accept by typing their name. The proposal records who accepted and when, and you can convert it into an invoice right away or automatically.

Does the PDF look like the online proposal? Yes. The PDF follows the editor layout: covers run to the edge of the page, page breaks are where you put them, and the signature area prints with signature and date lines.

Can I personalise each proposal? Yes. The customer’s name, billing address, and your company details fill in on their own, and saved blocks let you reuse sections you write once.

Will I know when a customer reads it? Yes. Proposals record when they were last viewed, and ready-made automations can alert the sales agent on the first view, follow up when a proposal is unopened or viewed, and remind before it expires.
