## Start from a template, not a blank page

Each template is a complete proposal for its industry, with its own colours, fonts, imagery, and copy you make yours. Hover the page to read it to the end.

- Web design
- Marketing
- IT services
- Consulting
- Construction & renovation
- Events & photography

26 blocks · 9 fonts

## Design the proposal, then every block

Write the proposal on the page it will be read on. Type / to add any of 26 blocks, set your colours and font once for the whole proposal, then fine-tune any block on its own.

- Layout: image position, alignment, and stretching to the edges
- Style: image shape, background, pattern, border, and text
- Spacing: padding and the space below each block
- Accent, nine fonts, and corners for the whole proposal in the Design tab

Optional items · accept online

## Customers pick the extras and accept online

Customers open a page that looks like your proposal, not like your CRM. They tick the optional items they want, watch the total update, and accept by typing their name.

- Mark rows optional and let the customer decide
- Accepted by name, with the time recorded, then locked
- A PDF that matches the page, with only the items they kept
- Files you share appear right on the proposal page

Automations · AI

## It follows up while you sell

Proposals get the same automations as your quotes: an alert the first time a customer opens one, follow-ups when it goes quiet, a reminder before it expires, and an invoice the moment it is accepted.
