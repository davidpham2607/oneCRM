Does oneCRM include project management? Yes. Projects, phases, tasks, and time tracking live in the same CRM as your customers and invoices, so every project is linked to the customer it is for.

Can I bill a project from tracked time? Yes. Track billable hours with timers or manual entries, then invoice billable tasks hourly or at a fixed price without leaving the project.

Can I reuse a project setup? Save phases, team members, tasks, and billing settings as a template. When you start a project from it, task due dates are scheduled from the new start date.

Can clients see how their project is going? Yes, if you want them to. Through the customer portal you choose what clients see about progress and deliverables.
