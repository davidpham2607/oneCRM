## You are the data controller

oneCRM is self-hosted software. The business that installs it, stores customer data on its servers, and decides how that data is used is the data controller under GDPR  -  not the software vendor. oneCRM provides features that help you honour data subject rights, but legal compliance is your responsibility. We recommend working with legal counsel to determine what applies to your business.

- Data stays on hardware you choose and control

- Configure privacy settings to match your policies and jurisdiction

- Step-by-step setup in the Privacy & GDPR admin documentation

Policy, timestamp, and IP on every acceptance

## Proof of consent you can show auditors

Every privacy policy acceptance on lead forms, support ticket forms, and customer portal registration is logged automatically  -  with the policy URL, context, timestamp, IP address, and browser. Customers can review their consent history in the portal Privacy & Data page.

- Automatic logging when forms require policy acknowledgement

- Full audit trail per contact and lead

- Consent history visible to customers in the portal
