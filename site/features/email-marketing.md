## Replies come straight back to the CRM

Connect a mailbox to the email inbox of your CRM and replies to your campaigns and sequences are picked up straight from it. They stop the sequence, count as replies, and sit in the conversation they belong to. No inbound email to set up at your provider.

- The reply-to field suggests your personal and shared mailboxes

- Sequences sent as each owner bring replies to that owner’s inbox

- Any email from the person counts as a reply, so stop-on-reply just works

Marketing works on its own too: replies can come in through your sending provider instead. Campaigns and sequences always go out through your sending provider, never through a personal mailbox.

Explore the email inbox

Is Marketing included in oneCRM? No. Marketing is a separately sold module from the oneCRM marketplace. It installs into your oneCRM, and each module licence is used with one Next licence.

Do I need an email sending provider? Yes. Marketing email goes out through your own Postmark, Mailgun, or Resend account, which keeps bulk sending off your server and your team’s mailboxes. You can connect several and choose one per campaign or sequence.

How is revenue attributed to an email? Won deals and fully paid invoices are credited to the last marketing email the customer clicked or replied to, within a credit window you set. Refunds, credit notes, and edits to the deal or invoice keep the figures right.

Does it help with GDPR? Every subscriber has a status and a legal basis, consent is recorded with the time, IP address, and the exact wording they agreed to, double opt-in is built in, and every email carries a one-click unsubscribe. With the Privacy features you can also export and erase a person’s data.

Do I need to set up inbound email for replies? No. Replies to your campaigns and sequences are picked up from the mailboxes you connect to the email inbox, which is part of oneCRM, so they stop sequences and count as replies without setting up inbound email at your provider.

Can it track website visits? Yes, if you add the tracking script to your site. It asks visitors for consent or follows Cookiebot, CookieYes, Complianz, or OneTrust, and ties a visit to a person once they click one of your emails or submit a form.
