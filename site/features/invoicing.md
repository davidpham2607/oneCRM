## Design the invoice PDF, block by block

Start from the built-in template and make it yours: move the logo, addresses, and line items, add your own text and images, then set the colours and font once. Preview it with a real invoice before you save, and use it for every invoice or only some.

- A page header and footer on every page, with merge tags and page numbers
- Layout: the footer’s height, the space above it, and hiding it on the first page
- Style: a background for the footer, sections, and other blocks
- Accent, nine fonts, and corners for the whole template in the Design tab

## Let customers pay the moment they open an invoice

Send a branded invoice with a public payment link and accept payments online through Stripe, PayPal and Mollie. Record manual and bulk payments, handle refunds, and apply surcharges automatically.

- Stripe, PayPal and Mollie online payments with webhooks
- Public invoice links and PDF downloads
- Single, bulk, and partial payment recording
- Refunds and automatic payment surcharges

## See how a customer usually pays before you invoice

Check whether a customer usually pays early, on time, or late while you create their invoice. The summary uses past payments and appears when there is enough history to help you plan.

- Instant insight when you select a customer or contact
- Late-payer warnings when more than half of past invoices were paid after the due date
- On-time confirmation when they consistently pay early or on the due date
- Shown only when there is enough payment history to be meaningful

## Invoice on a schedule, without the monthly busywork

Turn a regular charge into a recurring invoice and oneCRM creates each invoice on schedule  -  kept as a draft to review, or issued and emailed to the customer. Credit notes keep adjustments clean.

- Repeat every set number of days, weeks, months, or years
- A fixed number of cycles, or no end date
- Created as drafts, or issued and emailed automatically
- Credit notes applied to outstanding invoices
