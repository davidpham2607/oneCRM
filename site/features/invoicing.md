Runs on infrastructure you control  -  self-hosted CRM with a one-time licence.

Does oneCRM include invoicing? Yes. Invoicing is built into the CRM, next to your customers, quotes, and projects. Send a branded invoice with a public payment link and record single, bulk, or partial payments.

Can customers pay invoices online? Yes. Customers pay online through Stripe, PayPal or Mollie from the invoice link. You can also offer bank transfer and other payment methods with clear instructions. Need another provider? A developer can add one as a module.

Does it support recurring invoices? Yes. Repeat an invoice every set number of days, weeks, months, or years, for a fixed number of cycles or with no end date. Each new invoice can stay a draft for review, or be issued and emailed to the customer automatically. Customers pay each invoice themselves; cards are not charged automatically.

Does oneCRM send payment reminders? Yes. Due-date reminders and overdue notices go out automatically. Invoices in other currencies use ECB or custom exchange rates.
