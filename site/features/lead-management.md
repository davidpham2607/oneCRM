Which lead statuses does oneCRM use? Every new lead starts in New. Move it to Contacted and Qualified, or mark it Unqualified or Lost with a reason. You can rename, reorder, and add your own statuses.

How do leads get into the CRM? Leads arrive from embeddable web forms on your website, through Zapier from the other apps you use, from a CSV import, or when you add them yourself. Form submissions check for duplicates, so an existing lead can be skipped or updated.

Can I see which lead sources bring customers? Yes. Each lead keeps its source, and source performance reports show which channels actually produce customers. The conversion funnel shows where leads stall.

What happens when a lead is qualified? Convert it into a contact, a company, and a deal in one step, without typing their details again. The deal then moves through your sales pipeline.
