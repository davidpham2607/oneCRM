## Views that show who is waiting on you

Four views answer the question that matters at 9 a.m.: who is waiting on me?

- Needs reply: the customer wrote last
- Awaiting response: you wrote last, over two days ago
- Not linked to a record: senders who are not in your CRM yet
- My deals: every conversation on an open deal you own

Matched by address

## Every email files itself to the right contact and deal

Every email is matched by address to its contact, company, or lead. When the contact has one open deal, it links to that deal too.

The conversation then shows on each of those records, and unknown senders can become contacts automatically.

- The deal and the contact, linked to the conversation
- Open deals: change the stage, mark it won, or start a new one
- Next tasks for the customer, and one click to add another

Assign · message · snooze · done

## One sales@ inbox the whole team can work

Connect sales@ as a shared mailbox and choose the team that works it.

Each conversation has a status and an owner, so nothing is answered twice and nothing is left behind.

- Open, Snoozed, or Done: a new message reopens it
- Assign it to a colleague or another team, or let new ones go round in turn
- Internal messages with @mentions that the customer never sees
