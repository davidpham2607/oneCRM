Is the email inbox included in oneCRM? Yes. The email inbox is part of oneCRM. There is no add-on to buy and no extra licence.

Does it work with Outlook? Yes. Connect your Outlook mailbox and your email appears inside oneCRM, next to the contact, company, and open deals. You read and reply from the CRM, and what you send and receive still shows in Outlook.

Which mailboxes can I connect? Gmail and Outlook, once you connect Google or Microsoft in the CRM settings, and any other mailbox that supports IMAP. Each one can be personal or shared with your team.

How often does it sync? Both ways, about every three minutes, as long as the CRM’s scheduled tasks are running. The first import brings in up to six months of history.

Can my team share one inbox? Yes. Connect a mailbox such as sales@ as a shared mailbox and choose its team. Conversations can be assigned to a colleague or handed to another team, internal messages with mentions stay with the team, and a role permission decides which shared conversations each person sees.

How are emails linked to contacts and deals? By address. A message from a known contact, company, or lead links to it, and to the contact’s deal when they have exactly one open deal. You can link or unlink any conversation and create a lead from it.

Does it track opens and clicks? Yes, for email you send from the CRM. Your team’s own views are not counted. Like any open tracking, opens are not seen when the recipient’s email app blocks images.

Can AI write my replies? With AI set up in the CRM, Draft with AI writes a reply from the thread and the records it is linked to, and Summarize thread turns a long thread into a few lines. A permission controls who can use it.
