# Reports and dashboards

<div v-pre>


This page explains how to use reports in oneCRM: the built-in reports, building your own, and arranging dashboards.
Every report counts only the records the viewer may see, so the same report shows a sales rep their own deals and a manager their team's.

## Built-in reports

Modules ship reports for their records, for example revenue by month for invoices, win rate by owner for deals, and conversions by source for leads. Find them under **Reports**, grouped by module. Anyone who can view a module's records sees its reports; there is no separate reports permission.

A few reports, such as the lead and deal conversion funnels and invoice aging, are fixed pages. All others open in the report builder.

## Building a report

Click **New report** and choose what the report is about: invoices, deals, tickets, or any other record type, including custom fields. Then choose:

| Option | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Group by** | One row per value, such as per month, per status, or per owner. Choose **Nothing** for totals only. |
| **Split by** | Shows each row separately per a second grouping, such as each month per status |
| **Values** | What each row shows: a count, a total, an average, the lowest or highest value, a count of unique values, or one value as a percentage of another |
| **Filters** | The same filters as the record list |
| **Period** | Counts only records whose date falls in a range, such as this month or last quarter |
| **Sorting** | The order of the rows and how many to show |
| **Show as** | Table, bar chart, line chart, area chart, donut chart, or a single number |

Money is totalled in your base currency. Changes to a built-in report can be saved as your own copy. Share a report with everyone, or export its rows as CSV.

## Dashboards

You can have several dashboards: your own, and those shared with everyone. Mark the one that opens first. On a new account, the first dashboard starts with a short selection of key figures, lists and charts; the other widgets and built-in reports are under Add widget.

Click **Arrange widgets** to drag widgets by their handle, resize them, or remove them. **Add widget** adds a widget or any report you can see. A report can be added more than once, for example for two periods, and each copy can have its own title, period, and chart type. From a report, **Add to dashboard** pins it to one of your dashboards.
