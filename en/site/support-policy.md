Every paid licence includes ticket support for a set period: 1 year for oneCRM, and 6 months for oneCRM Classic website licences. Support is per product, so a module licence covers tickets about that module only.

## What support includes

- Answers to questions about how the product's features work.
- Help with bugs and errors in the product.
- Help with third-party libraries bundled with the product.
- Fixes for confirmed bugs, delivered through updates and patches.

## What support does not include

- Installing the product or setting up your server.
- Hosting, server, PHP, or database configuration.
- Customisation or custom development, including features beyond what the product offers.
- Problems caused by your own code changes.
- General PHP or framework help.
- Free modules.

## Before you open a ticket

- Update to the latest version. The issue may already be fixed.
- Check the [documentation](/en/docs/1.x/introduction).
- Make sure your server meets the [requirements](/en/docs/1.x/requirements).

## How to get support

Sign in and open a ticket from the [Contact](/en/site/contact) page. Include the steps to reproduce the issue and any error messages. Tickets are our only support channel.

## Response time

We reply within 5 business days, usually sooner.

## Third-party modules

The vendor named on a module listing supports that module under its own terms. We can help route your request.

## When support ends

You can keep using the version you have. Renew your licence to open tickets again. You can still report bugs from inside oneCRM with any valid licence.

## CodeCanyon purchases

Licences bought on CodeCanyon get the same support, for the period included with or extended on CodeCanyon.
