Announcements lets you post a short notice, such as "Office closed on Friday" or "Planned maintenance on Sunday night", to your staff, to your customers in the customer portal, or to both. It is free and needs no other modules.

## Where people see it

- **Staff** see it in the notices bar under the top bar, on every admin page. The bar shows the title, and **Details** opens the full message.
- **Customer portal contacts** see it at the top of the portal home page, with the full message.
- Each person sees it until they dismiss it or until it ends. Dismissing it hides it for that person only.

## Choose who sees it and when

- **Staff:** all staff, selected teams (the members and the manager of each team), or no staff
- **Customer portal:** show it to contacts too, or only to them
- **Type:** Info for news, or Warning for something people should act on, which turns the bar amber
- **Starts and Ends:** schedule it ahead of time and let it stop showing on its own, or show it right away until everyone dismisses it
- **Message:** bold, italic, lists, quotes and links

## Keep track of them

The announcements list shows each announcement's audience, start, end and status: Scheduled, Active or Ended. Click a title to edit it, and the change applies right away. Only staff with the **Manage announcements** permission can create, edit and delete announcements.
